Wwise (the sound of the game)
-----------------------------

Rocket Arena plays its sounds through Wwise, the sound engine of Audiokinetic Inc.

    Wwise (R) is (c) Audiokinetic Inc. All rights reserved. Used under license from Audiokinetic Inc.
    https://www.audiokinetic.com
